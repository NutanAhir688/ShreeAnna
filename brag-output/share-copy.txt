=== LINKEDIN ===
We built ShreeAnna to solve one of the hardest challenges in Indian agriculture: fair, transparent, and direct market access for millet farmers.

Until now, smallholder farmers faced fragmented supply chains, opaque quality testing, and delayed payouts. ShreeAnna rebuilds the procurement lifecycle end-to-end:

🌾 Cadastral Geo-Tagging: Precise farm parcel boundary mapping linked to registered farmers.
🔍 Real-Time Quality Grading: Certified moisture and impurity scoring with Akka.NET actor-isolated support chat between farmers and inspectors.
🚚 Transparent Farm-to-Silo Logistics: GPS telemetry and a 6-digit mutual handshake verification code.
⚡ Automated Direct Bank Settlements: Immediate disbursements with itemized deductions and UTR tracking.

Powered by .NET 10, Akka.NET, a modern web portal, and a Flutter mobile experience for on-ground operations.
